Buffalo or reservations?

Which response reaches Level 4?

Both writers know that the buffalo mattered. Look for the difference between explaining one factor and showing how the two factors connect.

The question

QUESTION “The destruction of the buffalo was the main reason why the traditional Native American way of life came to an end.” How far do you agree?

Response A: what level?

Of the two I think that destruction of the buffalo is the more important reason why the traditional way of life came to an end. One of the main reasons destruction of the buffalo caused the traditional Native American way of life to come to an end is because the Native Americans relied on the buffalo to live. For example, they used buffalo for meat, their skins to make tipis, their fur to make clothes and their hooves to make arrow heads etc... This contributed towards causing the traditional Native American way of life to come to an end because without the main animal they follow as a part of their nomadic lifestyle, they have nothing to live off as a part of their tradition.

However, reservation systems was less important in causing the traditional Native American way of life to come to an end because they were confined to an unfamiliar setting.

Your prediction: Level ___ / 12. Where does the response stop developing its explanation?

Response B: what level?

Destruction of the buffalo is the more significant factor in the ending of Native traditional life. Millions of buffalo were killed either for their resources to soldiers or for sport. This had a devastating effect on the Native American culture as their nomadic lifestyle was based majorly around the movement of the buffalo. However this was not the main reason for the end of traditional life, although it contributed massively. As the Natives were starving, without food from the buffalo, they were forced onto reservations as a way of survival.

The reservation system is the main reason for the destruction of Indian culture. Now under control of the government, many core elements of Native life were stripped away, such as polygamy and religious practices, to distance them further from traditional ways. As well as that, the government tried to civilise them to completely strip away any trace of Native culture, children were taken away to be educated as to the white mans way of life, in an attempt to faze out native culture completely.

The destruction of the buffalo in its self is not the main reason for the ending of Native way of life, however it did have a significant impact on forcing the Indians into the reservations which were the most important reason. Therefore without the killing of the buffalo the Natives wouldn't have lost their culture, which was inflicted on the reservations.

Your prediction: Level ___ / 12. Find the sentence that links the two factors together.

Reveal the verdicts and the difference

Response A: Level 2

It explains the buffalo’s importance, but the reservation point is only asserted. It needs to show what changed: no hunting, forced farming, children taken to boarding schools, and cultural practices restricted.

Response B: Level 4

It explains both factors and reaches a sustained judgement. Most importantly, it links them: buffalo destruction created starvation, which forced people onto reservations, where government policy attacked culture directly.

The one move to borrow

Link the factors, don’t leave them in separate paragraphs. Try: “This mattered because the destruction of the buffalo made the reservation system more effective: starving families had little choice but to accept government control.”
