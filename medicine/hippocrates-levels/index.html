The question

QUESTION Explain the significance of Hippocratic and Galenic medicine after c1000 AD. [8 marks]

For Level 4: explain the relationship between significances over time. Hippocratic and Galenic medicine began as a rational approach, but Church support turned it into an orthodoxy that held back later progress.

Response A: predict the level

Hippocratic & Galenic played a huge role in history, and especially after 1000 AD. The work of Hippocrates & Galen has been used throughout time on multiple occasions, for example, the encouragement of dissection, which Galen influenced, led to the discovery and depth of the human anatomy. While Galen himself didn’t accurately get everything right, his work and finding led to other peoples discoveries, like Vesalius creating his accurate book ‘The Fabric of the Human Body’. With this, we can recognise the part that Galen played in the accurate discovery of anatomy.

Galen’s work was worshiped in the medical era, since he always linked his ideas back to God which the Christian Church loved, so his knowledge & teachings were carried on for many years. However the errors in Galen’s work made people, like Harvey curious and ambitious enough to correct it. Harvey corrected Galen on the blood cycle, and how blood is actually pumped around the body, so by proving this proved Galen’s theory of opposites / bleeding treatment, completely useless, allowing an advancement in medicine. While it seems Galen’s research did nothing, without Galen’s influence & previous teachings, someone like Harvey wouldn’t of argued against it and proven it wrong.

Your prediction: Level ___ / 8. Where is the clearest explanation of significance?

Response B: predict the level

Hippocrates’ work is significant throughout history. His theory of the 4 humours was taught up to the Renaissance where his and Galens ideas were challenged. The theory of the 4 humours was supported by natural explanations, therefore he showed a greater and more scientific understanding about causes of disease than others in Greek medicine as others around him believed in supernatural causes. He also established the Hippocratic Oath, which is a promise made by doctors to ensure their patients well being. This oath is still taken today showing Hippocrates significant impact on medicine throughout history.

Galen developed Hippocrates ideas further and created the theory of opposites. This gave rational, logical and non supernatural treatment of disease as he suggested to treat a cold you must give the patient chillies to warm them. Galen experimented on animals and recorded his findings and he proved the brain controlled your voice and not the heart in his famous pig experiment. Galen was significant throughout history mainly because he believed that the human body was perfect and therefore had to be a creator (God) and this made the Christian church adopt his ideas and teach them throughout history.

Your prediction: Level ___ / 8. Identify two different significances.

Reveal the verdicts and the difference

Response A: Level 2

What is working: it has relevant knowledge and tries to use later examples.

Next step: stop retelling the story. Choose two clear answers to “why did this matter?” and explain each one.

Response B: Level 4

What is working: it develops two significances: rational explanations and the Church preserving Galen’s ideas.
