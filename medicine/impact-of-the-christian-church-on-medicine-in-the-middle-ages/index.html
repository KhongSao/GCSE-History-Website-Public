What happened & why it matters

  • Church adopts Galen (medieval)
    • At the time — gave stability to medical education
    • Long term — prevented anatomical progress for centuries
  • Council of Clermont (1130)
    • At the time — banned clergy from surgery
    • Long term — separated surgery from learned medicine; barber-surgeons emerged

Think like a historian

  • Can the same institution be both the most important provider of healthcare and the biggest obstacle to medical progress?
  • What does this tell us about the difficulty of judging historical institutions as simply “good” or “bad” — and how do we weigh the care they provided against the knowledge they suppressed?
